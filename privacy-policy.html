Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

This privacy policy explains how MyDay Apps ("we", "us") handles information in the Android app Daily Focus: Pomodoro Timer (package name com.myday.dailyfocus), referred to below as "the app".

Summary

  • The app does not require an account.
  • We do not collect, store or sell your personal information.
  • Your settings and timer history are stored only on your device.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your preferences, such as session and break lengths, and may save a record of completed sessions. This information is stored locally on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot access it.

You can delete this information at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information through the app. The app does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or other personal data.

Permissions

The app may ask for permission to show notifications, so it can tell you when a focus session or break has ended. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings. The app still works without this permission.

Third-party services

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information when you download or update the app, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive personal information about you from Google Play.

Contacting us

If you email us, we will receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use this only to reply to you, and we do not share it with anyone else. You can ask us to delete your emails at any time.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
