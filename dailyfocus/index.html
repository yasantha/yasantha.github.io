How it works

Three steps to a focused day

Pick one main focus

Choose the task that matters most today and tag it Work, Study or Personal. Everything else can wait.

Start a calm session

Set your Pomodoro length, layer rain or brown noise if you like, and work on that one thing until the timer ends.

Watch your jar fill

Every session drops a marble in your jar. Hit your daily goal and it glows. At the end of the week, see where your time went.

Features

Everything you need to focus, nothing you don't

Focus jar

A marble for every session, coloured by category. Reach your daily goal and the jar glows.

Pomodoro timer

Your own focus, break and long-break lengths. Breaks can start automatically, and +5 min is one tap away.

Never lose a session

Your real focus time is saved even if a call comes in or the app closes.

Add tasks in seconds

Type it, add a #tag, or just say it. Save routines for the sessions you repeat.

Focus sounds

Rain, ocean waves, fireplace, night crickets, white, pink and brown noise. Layer up to three.

Done early? Log real time

Finish before the timer ends and record the minutes you actually worked. No rounding up.

See where time goes

Week, month and year views, time by category and task, pace to your goal and your best time of day.

Streaks and consistency

A consistency map and streak counter show the days you showed up. Missed tasks can move to today.

Widget and Quick Settings tile

Start focusing from your home screen, share text straight into a task, and pick light or dark.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Is Daily Focus free?

Yes. Daily Focus is free to download on Google Play. It is supported by ads.

Is it available on iPhone?

Not yet. Daily Focus is an Android app on Google Play.

Can I change the Pomodoro timer lengths?

Yes. Set your own focus, short break and long break lengths, let breaks start automatically, and add 5 more minutes with one tap.

Which focus sounds are included?

Rain, ocean waves, fireplace, night crickets, white noise, pink noise and brown noise. Layer up to three, set the volume of each, and save a mix for a task.

What if a call comes in during a session?

Your real focus time is saved even if a call comes in or the app closes, so you keep the minutes you already worked.

Where are my tasks stored?

Your tasks and focus history are stored on your device. See the privacy policy for details about ads and data.

Which languages does it support?

English, French, German and Spanish.
